# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 27, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 27, 2026

## 1. Overview
AgriShield ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Crop Disease Detection & Phytopathology Decision Support System. We are committed to protecting the privacy, data security, and confidentiality of all agricultural producers, agronomists, researchers, and enterprise users.

## 2. Information We Collect
- **Foliar Photography:** Image files (.jpg, .jpeg, .png) submitted by users for pathology classification.
- **Micro-Climate Simulation Parameters:** Ambient temperature, relative humidity, and precipitation values configured during analysis.
- **Diagnostic Telemetry:** Computed vegetation indices (Excess Green, Green Leaf Index) and model latency metrics.
- **Technical Log Data:** Ephemeral server logs including request timestamps, user-agent headers, and HTTP status codes.

## 3. Ephemeral Processing & Data Retention
- All uploaded leaf photographs are processed strictly in volatile system memory (RAM).
- Uploaded leaf photographs are not retained, permanently stored, cataloged, or repurposed for external model training without explicit written consent.
- EXIF metadata (including embedded GPS geolocation coordinates and camera hardware identifiers) is automatically stripped prior to neural tensor normalization.

## 4. Third-Party Disclosures & Transfers
We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your agricultural imagery, farm coordinates, or diagnostic telemetry to any third-party advertisers, chemical distributors, or data brokers.

## 5. Security Measures
Our system employs industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS 1.3 for data in transit), memory sandboxing, and regular vulnerability scanning to prevent unauthorized access or interception.

## 6. User Rights & Regulatory Compliance
Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR and CCPA), you possess the right to:
- Inquire about data processing workflows.
- Request immediate termination of cached sessions.
- Submit inquiries regarding privacy practices.
